# Dataset status

`synthetic-pilot.jsonl` contains 20 **synthetic software fixtures**, not research
queries, reviewed gold, or empirical provider comparisons. They exercise ten domains
and the replay/report workflow without transmitting anything or spending credits.

`core-100-candidates.jsonl` is a curation queue of 100 information needs and primary
publisher starting points. It contains no approved gold or attested holdout labels.
Do not run or publish it as Core-100. Use the annotation guide to collect exact
visuals, source identities, precise locations and applicable datasets after a freeze.

No validated pilot or Core-100 release exists yet. Two actual human reviewers and
an Evidence8 freeze attestation are external prerequisites; the software cannot
legitimately manufacture them.
